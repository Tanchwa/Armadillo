Armadillo is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Opt a namespace in to startup gating:

  kubectl label namespace <namespace> {{ range $k, $v := .Values.webhook.namespaceSelector.matchLabels }}{{ $k }}={{ $v }} {{ end }}

Then bind workloads to profiles with a ProfileBinding. Repository Secrets must
carry the label armadillo.andrewsutliff.com/secret-type=repository, or the
manager will not see them.

Check that a loader is ready on every node:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get daemonset {{ include "armadillo.fullname" . }}-loader
